# Workout Plan

### DAY 1 — PUSH (Chest Emphasis)

- **Dumbbell floor press** — 4x10
- **Dumbbell chest fly (floor)** — 3x12
- **Push-ups with band across back** — 3x max reps
- **Dumbbell shoulder press** — 3x12
- **Dumbbell lateral raise** — 3x15
- **Band tricep pushdown** — 3x12
- **Chair dips** — 3x12

---

### DAY 2 — PULL (Back Width + Biceps)

- **Dumbbell bent-over row** — 4x10
- **Band lat pulldown (door anchor)** — 4x12
- **Band seated row (band around feet)** — 3x12
- **Band face pull** — 3x15
- **Dumbbell bicep curl** — 3x12
- **Dumbbell hammer curl** — 3x12
- **Core:** Leg raises 3x15 + Plank 3x45s

---

### DAY 3 — LEGS (Heavy)

- **Dumbbell goblet squat** — 4x12
- **Dumbbell Romanian deadlift** — 4x10
- **Dumbbell walking lunge** — 3x12 each leg
- **Bulgarian split squat (foot on chair)** — 3x10 each leg
- **Dumbbell glute bridge** — 3x15
- **Calf raises** — 3x20

---

### DAY 4 — PUSH (Shoulder Emphasis)

- **Dumbbell Arnold press** — 4x10
- **Dumbbell floor press** — 3x12
- **Dumbbell lateral raise** — 4x15
- **Dumbbell front raise** — 3x12
- **Band chest fly** — 3x12
- **Dumbbell overhead tricep extension** — 3x12
- **Core:** Russian twist (dumbbell) 3x20 + Plank 3x45s

---

### DAY 5 — PULL (Back Thickness + Arms)

- **Dumbbell single-arm row** — 4x12 each side
- **Band lat pulldown** — 3x12
- **Band pull-apart** — 3x15
- **Dumbbell concentration curl** — 3x12
- **Band hammer curl** — 3x15
- **Band reverse curl** — 3x15
- **Core:** Bicycle crunches 3x20 + Leg raises 3x15

---

### DAY 6 — LEGS (Light) + FULL CORE

- **Dumbbell sumo squat** — 3x15
- **Dumbbell step-up (chair)** — 3x12 each leg
- **Band squat** — 3x20
- **Band lateral walk** — 3x15 each direction
- **Band glute bridge** — 3x20
- **Core circuit (2 rounds):** Plank 45s → Crunches 20 → Leg raises 15 → Mountain climbers 30s → Band pallof press 12 each side

---

### DAY 7 — REST

Full rest. A walk is fine, nothing structured.
